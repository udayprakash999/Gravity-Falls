Welcome to Gravity Falls

Discover the mysterious town of Gravity Falls, where strange creatures, supernatural secrets, and unexplained adventures await. Join Dipper and Mabel Pines as they uncover hidden mysteries and explore the secrets behind the Mystery Shack.

Characters

Stan Pines

Meet Stan! He runs the Mystery Shack, loves money, and has lots of funny secrets.

Bill Cipher

A tricky dream demon with one eye. He loves causing trouble and wants to take over Gravity Falls!

Dipper Pines

He is a smart and brave boy who loves solving mysteries with his twin sister Mabel

GRUNKLE FORD

He is a smart scientist who studies strange creatures and mysteries in Gravity Falls.

Mabel Pines

She is a fun, happy girl who loves adventures, colorful sweaters, and solving mysteries with Dipper.

Wendy Corduroy

She is a cool, fun-loving teenager who works at the Mystery Shack and loves going on adventures.

Pacifica Northwest

She is a rich and popular girl who loves fashion but learns to be kind and make new friends.
